How to Use AI for Files & Documents

Summarize, extract, and make sense of anything you upload — starting from the very basics.

Aiverture · Updated July 2026 · 5 min read

A 40-page PDF, a dense contract, a wall of meeting notes — these are exactly the situations where AI saves the most real time. You upload it, ask a question, and get a direct answer instead of reading the whole thing yourself.

What AI can actually do with a document

Most AI chatbots today let you upload a file directly into the chat — PDFs, Word docs, spreadsheets, even photos of physical pages. Once it's uploaded, you can ask it anything about the content, the same way you'd ask a person who already read it for you.

Real examples to try

Quick summary

"Summarize this PDF in 3 bullet points."

Extracting specifics

"Pull out every date and deadline mentioned in this contract."

Plain-English translation

"Explain this legal document to me like I've never read one before."

Finding what matters

"What are the most important takeaways from these meeting notes?"

How to get better results

  • Ask a specific question, not just "summarize this." "What does this contract say about cancellation?" gets a sharper answer than a generic summary.
  • Ask it to quote the exact section. For anything important, ask where in the document it found the answer — makes it easy to double-check.
  • Break up huge documents. If it's extremely long, ask about one section at a time for more focused answers.
Worth knowing: for legal, medical, or financial documents, treat AI's explanation as a helpful starting point, not a substitute for a professional's review when real decisions are on the line. And avoid uploading highly sensitive personal documents (like ones with full ID numbers) unless you understand the tool's privacy policy first.

Your first thing to try

Find one document you've been meaning to actually read — a bill, a form, anything — and upload it with one question: "what do I actually need to know here?"
